Methodology03
Phase 1 · Source

Don't reinvent — curate from what already exists

Ten public text-to-SQL datasets, merged into one canonical training set:

  • b-mc2/sql-create-context
  • gretelai/synthetic_text_to_sql
  • knowrohit07/know_sql
  • NumbersStation/NSText2SQL
  • Clinton/Text-to-sql-v1
  • motherduckdb/duckdb-text2sql-25k
  • bugdaryan/spider-natsql-wikisql
  • ChrisHayduk/Llama-2-SQL
  • kaxap/llama2-sql-instruct
  • PipableAI/spider-bird

The question we faced:

"Should we build our own dataset, or assemble from public sources?"

Building from scratch would have taken weeks and produced a smaller, lower-quality corpus. The internet had already done most of the work.

Combined raw size: ~1.2 million rows. Combined quality: uneven. Schemas inconsistent. Duplicates plentiful.

Phase 4 · Train · choice of tooling

Fine-tune a 7B model on a single GPU — without paying $1,000

The decision point:

"Vanilla transformers on a 4× A100 cluster, or Unsloth on one L40S?"

Unsloth uses 4-bit quantization + LoRA + custom Triton kernels. Same training math, dramatically less memory and time.

The trick that mattered: packing=True in TRL. Concatenated 723k examples into 154k sequences of 1024 tokens. 4× speedup. ~$14 saved.
